## Scope, Method, and Limits

This file underwrites all six funded holdings, Discovery's five fresh priorities, and the four active or conflicted Ledger candidates. The broker-verified funded set is **NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, TSLA, IREN, and WULF**. Cash is **€5,487.36** and account value is **€9,327.53**. Prices are off-hours and **DATA LIMITED**, so no execution claim is made.

Each survival score is a **CAOS INFERENCE**, not a statistical default probability. It combines liquidity, self-funding, contract quality, leverage, dilution, and operational proof. Each fresh-buy weight is a **standalone maximum recommendation**, not an additive portfolio or an order. The Portfolio Court owns final sizing and portfolio-count decisions.

The two required lenses stay separate:

- **Raw convexity:** the security's possible 3x/5x/10x path before survival and dilution adjustments.
- **Survivability-adjusted attractiveness:** the quality of evidence, balance-sheet durability, per-share economics, and probability of reaching the path.
