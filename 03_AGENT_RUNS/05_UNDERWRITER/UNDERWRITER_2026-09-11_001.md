## Monster File 1 — Credo Technology (CRDO)

### Denominator, per-share economics, financing, and durability

- **VERIFIED FACT:** CRDO reported fiscal Q1 2027 revenue of **$479.0m**, up **9.6% sequentially** and **114.7% year over year**. GAAP gross margin was **64.5%**, GAAP net income **$129.4m**, and diluted EPS **$0.67**.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** Fiscal Q2 guidance is revenue of **$525m-$535m**, GAAP gross margin of **62.9%-64.9%**, and non-GAAP gross margin of **67%-69%**.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** Cash and short-term investments were **$764.3m**. The current evidence shows profitability and liquidity, so near-term corporate survival is not the main risk.
- **DATA LIMITED:** The off-hours price near **$160.31** implies about **$31.2bn** common equity value. Against Q2 guided annualized revenue of about $2.12bn, that is roughly **14.7x run-rate sales**. Annualization is not a forecast.
- **DATA LIMITED:** Customer concentration, product-level mix, fully diluted share count, and durability after the current AI-cluster ramp require deeper normalization. The Industry file supports rising networking and optics content, but also warns that copper, pluggable optics, co-packaged optics, and proprietary fabrics can shift value rapidly.
- **CAOS INFERENCE:** CRDO owns a real AI connectivity bottleneck with strong current economics, but the security price already assumes sustained high growth and premium margins. The investment case depends on per-share durability, not merely continued AI spending.

### RAW-CONVEXITY REGIME — SEPARATE FIELD

- **3x:** **POSSIBLE, NOT YET BASE-CASE PROVEN.** Equity value would need to reach about **$93.5bn**. At 10x sales, annual revenue would need to approach **$9.4bn**; at 15x, about **$6.2bn**.
- **5x:** **LOW-TO-MODERATE CREDIBILITY.** About **$155.8bn** equity value. At 12x sales it requires roughly **$13.0bn** annual revenue and sustained premium margins.
- **10x:** **LOW CREDIBILITY.** About **$311.6bn**. It requires durable leadership across several interconnect generations, much broader customer scale, and no severe multiple compression.
- **30x / 100x:** **NOT CREDIBLE ON CURRENT EVIDENCE.**

### EVIDENCE / SURVIVABILITY-ADJUSTED ATTRACTIVENESS — SEPARATE FIELD

**MODERATE-TO-GOOD.** Corporate survivability is **GOOD** because CRDO is profitable and liquid. Execution evidence is **GOOD** for the next quarter because Q2 guidance is explicit and above Q1 revenue. Security attractiveness is reduced by the roughly 14.7x run-rate-sales denominator, unclosed customer concentration, and technology substitution risk. Evidence quality is **HIGH** for reported results and **DATA LIMITED** for long-run per-share economics.

**Time required:** a credible 3x likely requires **4-7 years** of high growth with durable margins. **CAOS INFERENCE.**

### Explicit kill conditions

1. Fiscal Q2 2027 revenue falls below **$525m** without a clearly temporary timing explanation.
2. GAAP gross margin falls below **60%** for two consecutive quarters while revenue continues to grow.
3. A major customer loss or product transition causes sequential revenue contraction for two quarters.
4. Fully diluted share growth exceeds **10%** over twelve months without matching per-share earnings growth.
5. Operating cash flow materially trails GAAP profit for two consecutive quarters because receivables, inventory, or acquisition costs absorb earnings.
6. Copper/optical or scale-up architecture shifts reduce CRDO content per AI system and management cannot replace the lost economics.

## Monster File 2 — Modine Manufacturing (MOD)

### Denominator, per-share economics, financing, and durability

- **VERIFIED FACT:** Fiscal Q1 2027 sales were **$874.1m**, up **28%**, and EPS was **$1.37**, up **44%**. Data Centers segment sales rose **90%** to **$348.6m**.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** Data Centers gross margin fell from **29.8% to 20.2%**, a **960-basis-point** decline, because of expansion costs, supply constraints, higher material costs, production inefficiency, and a prior-year warranty benefit.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** Q1 operating cash flow was **$41.4m** and free cash flow was **negative $5.0m**. Total debt was **$528.2m**, cash **$95.3m**, and net debt **$432.9m**.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** Management retained fiscal 2027 sales-growth guidance of **20%-35%** and adjusted EBITDA of **$650m-$680m**. - **DATA LIMITED:** The off-hours price near **$179.26** implies about **$9.68bn** equity value, roughly 2.8x a simple Q1 annualized revenue base. Segment backlog quality and the cancellation, concentration, pricing, and capital terms behind the announced $4bn cooling agreement remain incomplete.
- **CAOS INFERENCE:** MOD offers the best combination in this set of a smaller denominator and verified physical-bottleneck revenue. The central issue is whether rapid data-center growth creates per-share cash economics or merely consumes capacity capital while margins compress.

- **5x:** **POSSIBLE TAIL.** About **$48.4bn** equity value. It requires global category leadership, strong cash conversion, and a continuing-company earnings base materially above current guidance.
- **10x:** **LOW CREDIBILITY.** About **$96.8bn**. Current revenue scale, debt, and margin evidence do not support this as a reasonable case.
- **30x / 100x:** **NOT CREDIBLE.**

### EVIDENCE / SURVIVABILITY-ADJUSTED ATTRACTIVENESS — SEPARATE FIELD

**MODERATE.** Corporate survivability is **GOOD-TO-MODERATE**: the company is profitable with positive operating cash flow, but net debt and negative quarterly free cash flow reduce flexibility during expansion. Raw convexity is stronger than CRDO or AXON because the denominator is smaller. Adjusted attractiveness remains only moderate until Data Centers margins recover and the post-separation company is normalized. Evidence quality is **HIGH** for current financials and **DATA LIMITED** for contract quality and continuing-company economics.

**Time required:** **4-7 years** for a credible 3x if cooling growth converts into margin and free cash flow. **CAOS INFERENCE.**

### Explicit kill conditions

1. Data Centers revenue growth falls below **25%** for two quarters before margins recover.
2. Data Centers gross margin remains below **22%** for two consecutive quarters after supply constraints ease.
3. Fiscal 2027 adjusted EBITDA guidance falls below **$650m** for structural reasons.
4. Free cash flow remains negative for three consecutive quarters while net debt rises.
5. The Performance Technologies separation is delayed beyond calendar Q1 2027 or leaves unexpected debt, liabilities, or stranded costs in continuing MOD.
6. The large cooling agreement is cancelled, materially delayed, or requires pricing that prevents acceptable segment margins.
