The Underwriter's core finding — that the specific July/August production-*start* claim, as literally guided, was missed and quietly replaced with vaguer language *before* today's event — stands independently of this correction and is itself VERIFIED FACT (Tesla's own Q2 letter).

## What Changed Today, Net

1. **Cybercab launched, but its regulatory path did not.** NHTSA has not approved unrestricted public operation of the controls-free design — this is the single most consequential open item, per the Underwriter's own assessment, and a real execution-risk marker (regulatory approvals of this kind historically resolve over quarters, not weeks).
2. **No finalized consumer pricing exists.** The sub-$30K aspiration predates this event; nothing new confirms it.
3. **Optimus production did start — late, and at training-pilot scale, not the revenue-relevant milestone.** The specific guided window was missed and quietly replaced by Tesla's own words before today's event, a fact independent of whether any production has since begun at all.
4. **Sell-side reaction was reported as skeptical** (Barclays, Piper Sandler, Morgan Stanley all reported as critical/underwhelmed per the Verifier's search), though the net price effect could not be reconciled from conflicting snippets — consistent with, not proof of, the evidentiary picture above. Per Risk's explicit instruction, this sentiment signal is not itself a break-gate trigger.
