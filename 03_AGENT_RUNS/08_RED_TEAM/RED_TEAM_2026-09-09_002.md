They also establish that ONDS has the smallest denominator and a credible, though unproven, 3x path. A decision system that requires every proof gate to close before any ownership can systematically buy strong businesses only after much of the asymmetry is visible and priced in.

**CAOS INFERENCE:** “Not buy-authorized” is a state label, not a return calculation. The Court correctly admits that it has no probability-weighted expected-CAGR ranking and no proof that cash maximizes returns. It then uses the absence of authorization to support cash. That risks circular reasoning: the candidates do not receive capital because they have not cleared the ownership burden, while the ownership burden is not expressed as a measurable edge over cash.

**CAOS INFERENCE:** ISRG presents the strongest challenge to cash. Its 40.6x trailing earnings multiple is demanding, but the upstream analysis does not model a normal compounding case below 3x. The Underwriter tests 3x, 5x, and 10x regimes but does not test a more ordinary 1.5x–2x outcome or compare that result with the expected cash return.

**CAOS INFERENCE:** The portfolio already holds 58.1% cash. This strongly protects survival, but it also means the marginal risk of a carefully limited new position could be modest. **CAOS INFERENCE:** Cash itself is not fully underwritten. No cash yield, inflation assumption, EUR purchasing-power effect, currency exposure, or reinvestment horizon is stated.
