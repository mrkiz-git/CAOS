## Strongest Opposing Case

### “Cash first” is process status, not proven expected-return result

- **VERIFIED FACT:** Broker display was before regular US trading. Court and Risk state executable prices, same-time EUR/USD, exact EUR weights, and transaction history are unavailable.
- **DATA LIMITED:** No report measures cash yield, inflation, tax, currency exposure, or required excess return for equity risk. No common valuation denominator exists for cash, incumbents, tracked candidates, or same-day referrals.
- **CAOS INFERENCE:** “No action while execution is blocked” is valid. “Cash is economic winner” is not proven. Missing comparison data cannot itself establish cash superiority.

### Incumbents face lower proof burden than referrals

- **VERIFIED FACT:** PLPC, BELFB, and TSSI are denied a capital claim because valuation, diluted-share, leverage, cash-flow, and concentration evidence is incomplete.
- **DATA LIMITED:** Court ranks MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA, CRDO, ISRG, AXON, and CEG ahead of all same-day referrals without a same-day Underwriter comparison of these fields on a common basis.
- **CAOS INFERENCE:** Ranking known names above new names may be sensible, but evidence chain does not prove it. This is potential incumbency bias.

### PLPC may not be strongest new referral

- **VERIFIED FACT:** PLPC reported 25% year-over-year sales growth and 34.3% gross margin. TSSI reported 46% systems-integration growth and 84% facilities-management growth. BELFB reported 25.1% sales growth, 39.9% gross margin, and a large equity raise.
- **DATA LIMITED:** PLPC backlog, cash conversion, customer concentration, leverage, valuation, and return on capacity spending are open. TSSI customer concentration, margins, financing, cash conversion, and valuation are open. BELFB dilution denominator and use of remaining proceeds are open.
- **CAOS INFERENCE:** PLPC has best closed profitable-quarter evidence. It does not have enough closed evidence to be declared strongest candidate. BELFB may have better balance-sheet improvement after dilution reconciliation; TSSI may have higher normalized per-share growth if customer economics close.

### Portfolio correlation argument lacks measurement

- **VERIFIED FACT:** Risk identifies overlap between owned AI/data-center exposure and TSSI, AAOI, VICR, BELFB, and parts of PLPC.
- **DATA LIMITED:** No beta, revenue-driver sensitivity, scenario loss, or factor model supports a 45% cluster threshold or ranks diversification benefit across PLPC, BELFB, TSSI, AMBA, AXON, CEG, ISRG, and cash.
- **CAOS INFERENCE:** A non-AI-capex exposure could improve portfolio expected value even if standalone growth is lower. Court does not test this on common data.

### Recycling conclusion exceeds available evidence

- **VERIFIED FACT:** Court keeps TSLA, IREN, and WULF recycling cases open. No sell is proposed.
- **DATA LIMITED:** Live weights, cost of sale, tax effect, spread, EUR/USD, and probability-weighted alternative returns are absent.
- **CAOS INFERENCE:** Case against adding new cash to these names is stronger than case for recycling existing capital. “High opportunity cost” is directionally plausible but not quantified enough for an allocation change.

## Exact Evidence That Reverses This Objection

Red Team accepts cash-first economic ranking only after all items below use one timestamp and one method:

1. Regular-session broker prices, authenticated EUR/USD, exact EUR values and weights, real-cash confirmation, and reconciled transactions.
2. Cash yield after tax and fees, inflation/currency exposure, plus minimum excess-return hurdle for equity risk.
3. Current price, market value, net cash/debt, diluted shares, normalized free cash flow, concentration, and valuation for MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA, CRDO, ISRG, AXON, CEG, PLPC, BELFB, TSSI, VICR, AAOI, and AMBA.
4. Common bear/base/upside expected-CAGR cases with per-share dilution, margin, free-cash-flow, terminal valuation, and failure sensitivity.
5. PLPC backlog/cancellation, organic-growth, working-capital, capacity-return, concentration, debt, and valuation closure.
6. TSSI customer share, contract protection, segment margin, cash conversion, funding, share count, and $17m investment-return closure.

If PLPC, BELFB, TSSI, or an omitted Discovery lead clears hurdle and outranks an incumbent or optionality holding, ranking must change.
